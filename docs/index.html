Finding 02The surprise: songs got longer first

We expected a steady decline. Instead hits grew from 2.7 minutes in the 1960s to 4.3 in the 1990s, and have only been shrinking since — they are still longer than the songs our parents grew up with.

TODO — Spotify is guessing too. Valence and energy are scores from Spotify's own model, not measurements of emotion. We found the same track scored differently on two different days, which tells you how firm these numbers are.
